# Alpinetel Unlimited Go — Current 2026 Product Snapshot

**Snapshot date:** July 2026  
**Status:** Current reference for competitive marketing

## Offer

Alpinetel Unlimited Go is marketed at **CHF 49/month**.

The package includes:
- unlimited data in Switzerland;
- unlimited calls within Switzerland;
- 5 GB of roaming data in EU countries per month;
- no included roaming data outside the EU;
- optional travel packages for selected non-EU destinations.

## Positioning

Alpinetel leads with a lower headline price and a strong “good value for everyday use” narrative.

Its main trade-off is lower included EU roaming data than Züricom and HelvetiTel.

## Customer perception

Züricom customer research suggests that price-sensitive customers may consider Alpinetel attractive, while frequent travelers who use substantial data abroad may value a more generous roaming allowance.

## Competitive implication

A price-only positioning against Alpinetel is unlikely to be persuasive for frequent travelers. A stronger comparison should focus on the relationship between monthly price, included roaming and simplicity.
