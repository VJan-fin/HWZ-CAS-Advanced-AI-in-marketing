# Züricom Family Mobile — Product Fact Sheet

**Owner:** Consumer Mobile Product Management  
**Version:** 2.2  
**Effective from:** 20 May 2026  
**Status:** Current

## Overview

Züricom Family Mobile is designed for households that want to manage multiple mobile lines under one account.

The standard family configuration starts with one primary subscription and allows additional mobile lines at a reduced monthly price, depending on the base plan.

## Family positioning

The main customer benefits are:
- one household account;
- simpler administration and billing;
- shared benefits on qualifying plans;
- predictable monthly pricing for additional lines.

## Relationship to Unlimited Plan

The Unlimited Plan can be combined with up to three additional Family SIMs at CHF 15/month per additional SIM.

The Family Mobile product should not be described as identical to the Unlimited Plan. Family Mobile is a broader commercial proposition covering several base-plan combinations.

## Marketing guidance

Useful audience language:
- “Keep the household connected under one account.”
- “Make managing several mobile subscriptions simpler.”

Avoid claiming that every Züricom mobile plan offers identical Family SIM terms. Product-specific conditions apply.
