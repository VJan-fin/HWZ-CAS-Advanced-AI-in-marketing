# HelvetiTel Unlimited Plus — Archived 2025 Product Snapshot

**Snapshot date:** September 2025  
**Status:** Archived reference — do not use for current pricing without checking a newer source.

## Offer at the time

HelvetiTel Unlimited Plus was advertised at **CHF 49/month** for a standard individual subscription.

The package included:
- unlimited mobile data in Switzerland;
- unlimited calls and SMS in Switzerland;
- **10 GB of roaming data in EU countries**;
- unlimited calls and SMS while roaming in EU countries;
- 1 GB of roaming data in selected countries outside the EU.

## Positioning at the time

HelvetiTel focused its 2025 messaging on price and broad everyday value. Campaigns frequently used phrases such as “simple unlimited mobile” and emphasized the lower monthly fee compared with premium Swiss providers.

## Competitive interpretation

In 2025, HelvetiTel's main apparent advantage against Züricom was the lower headline price. Its roaming offer was more limited than the current Züricom Unlimited Plan's EU roaming proposition.

## Important notice

This document is retained for historical campaign analysis only. Prices, roaming allowances and positioning can change. **Do not treat CHF 49 as HelvetiTel's current price.**
