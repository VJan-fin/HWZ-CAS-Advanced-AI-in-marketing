# Züricom Email Campaign Guidelines

**Owner:** CRM & Lifecycle Marketing  
**Version:** 2.1  
**Effective from:** 1 March 2026  
**Status:** Current

## Structure

Promotional B2C emails should normally contain:
1. a clear subject line;
2. an optional preheader that adds context rather than repeating the subject;
3. one main customer benefit;
4. supporting proof points;
5. one primary call to action.

## Subject lines

Target **35–50 characters** where practical. Avoid unnecessary punctuation and excessive urgency.

Good example:
> “Travel more, worry less about roaming”

Weak example:
> “🚨 DON'T MISS THIS INCREDIBLE ONCE-IN-A-LIFETIME DEAL!!!”

## Body copy

Keep promotional emails scannable. Short paragraphs and bullets are preferred over long blocks of text.

Use precise qualifiers for price, geography, eligibility and duration. If a product benefit depends on a condition, state the condition close to the claim.

## CTA

Use one primary CTA, such as “Check the plan” or “View offer”. Avoid competing calls to action.

## Compliance

Any claim about price, roaming coverage, duration, or eligibility must match the current product and claims documentation. Do not simplify “EU countries” to “Europe” if doing so changes the scope of the offer.
