# HelvetiTel Unlimited Plus — Current 2026 Product Snapshot

**Snapshot date:** August 2026  
**Status:** Current reference for competitive marketing

## Offer

HelvetiTel Unlimited Plus is marketed at **CHF 59/month** for an individual subscription.

The current offer includes:
- unlimited mobile data in Switzerland;
- unlimited calls and SMS in Switzerland;
- **20 GB of roaming data in EU countries** per month;
- 5 GB of roaming data in the USA and Canada;
- unlimited calls and SMS within the EU while roaming.

## Positioning

HelvetiTel has shifted its communication from price-led messaging toward a broader “travel-ready” proposition. Current campaign material emphasizes simplicity, international usability and flexibility.

The product is now at the same headline monthly price as Züricom Unlimited.

## Competitive interpretation

A simple “we are cheaper” position is no longer supported by the current price comparison.

For frequent travelers, HelvetiTel's strengths include a broad travel proposition and 5 GB of USA/Canada allowance, while its included EU roaming is more limited than Züricom's. Züricom's potential differentiators include the simplicity of its Swiss + EU proposition, Family SIM structure and clear cancellation terms.
