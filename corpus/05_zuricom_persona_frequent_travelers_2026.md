# Züricom Persona — Frequent Travelers

**Owner:** Consumer Marketing Insights  
**Version:** 1.3  
**Effective from:** 1 February 2026  
**Status:** Current

## Who they are

Frequent Travelers are customers who leave Switzerland regularly for work, city breaks, family visits or longer leisure trips. The segment includes both professionals and highly mobile private customers.

The segment is not defined by income alone. What matters is the frequency and diversity of travel and the customer's desire for simple connectivity while abroad.

## Main needs

Research identifies five recurring needs:
1. **Predictability:** customers want to avoid surprise roaming charges.
2. **Simplicity:** they do not want to study complicated country tables before every trip.
3. **Connectivity:** messaging, maps, bookings and work communication are seen as essential while travelling.
4. **Flexibility:** customers value being able to travel without changing their subscription repeatedly.
5. **Trust:** unclear qualifiers or promotional claims create skepticism.

## Pain points

Common frustrations include:
- uncertainty about whether a destination is included;
- fear of bill shock outside included zones;
- different rules between subscriptions;
- promotional prices that later revert to a much higher standard price;
- having to activate several travel options for different trips.

## Messaging implications

For this segment, lead with clarity and practical value rather than luxury or technical language.

Strong themes:
- “Know what is included before you travel.”
- “Stay connected without planning your mobile subscription around every trip.”
- “Simple travel connectivity.”

Avoid implying that all countries are treated the same. The audience values precision because roaming uncertainty is one of its pain points.
