# Züricom Travel Add-on — Product Note

**Owner:** Consumer Mobile Product Management  
**Version:** 1.7  
**Effective from:** 10 May 2026  
**Status:** Current

## Offer

The Züricom Travel Add-on provides included EU roaming benefits for **eligible mobile plans that do not already include the relevant roaming allowance**.

**Price:** CHF 10/month.

The option is billed monthly and can be removed after the minimum period defined in the customer's base subscription terms.

## Eligibility

The Travel Add-on is available on selected Züricom consumer plans. It is **not required for the current Unlimited Plan for normal EU travel**, because EU roaming is already included in that product.

Customers on plans without included EU roaming can use the add-on to obtain the applicable EU roaming allowance during the period in which the option is active.

## Positioning guidance

Good marketing language:
- “Add EU roaming to eligible plans for CHF 10/month.”
- “A simple roaming option for customers whose plan does not already include EU roaming.”

Avoid:
- “Unlimited Plan roaming add-on”;
- “CHF 10 to unlock EU roaming on every plan”;
- “Worldwide roaming add-on”.

## Relationship to Unlimited Plan

When creating customer-facing content for the Unlimited Plan, prioritize the included EU roaming benefit from the Unlimited Plan product sheet and roaming policy. Mention the Travel Add-on only when comparing plans or explaining how customers on other eligible plans can add EU roaming.
