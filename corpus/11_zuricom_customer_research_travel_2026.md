# Züricom Customer Research — Travel & Mobile Connectivity 2026

**Research owner:** Customer Insights  
**Fieldwork:** March–April 2026  
**Sample:** 1,240 Swiss mobile customers who travel abroad at least twice per year

## Executive summary

Frequent travelers are less motivated by a large number of telecom features than by **clarity about what will work abroad and what it will cost**.

### Finding 1 — Predictability beats feature volume

68% of respondents rated “knowing what I will pay before I travel” as very important. In open-ended responses, bill shock and uncertainty about roaming were frequent themes.

### Finding 2 — Geography creates confusion

Respondents often use “Europe” as a casual term even when they mean EU countries. When comparing mobile plans, however, customers react negatively when marketing language sounds broader than the actual country coverage.

### Finding 3 — Frequent travelers value simplicity

59% said they would prefer a plan with fewer decisions and clearly included travel benefits over a cheaper plan that requires several travel options.

### Finding 4 — Price still matters

Price remains a significant purchase factor, but respondents tend to accept a moderate premium when the additional value is concrete and easy to understand.

## Implications for marketing

For frequent travelers, prioritize:
1. predictable roaming;
2. clear geographic scope;
3. simple plan structure;
4. practical use cases such as maps, messaging and bookings.

Avoid highly technical descriptions of network architecture unless the audience explicitly asks for them.

## Research caution

This research describes customer perceptions and should not be used as proof of a product feature. Product and claims documentation remain the authority for factual offer details.
