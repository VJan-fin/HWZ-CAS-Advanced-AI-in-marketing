# Züricom Brand Voice & Messaging Guidelines

**Owner:** Brand & Customer Experience  
**Version:** 4.0  
**Effective from:** 15 April 2026  
**Status:** Current

## Brand personality

Züricom should sound:
- **clear** — customers should understand the offer quickly;
- **confident** — state real benefits directly;
- **human** — prefer natural language to telecom jargon;
- **useful** — explain why a benefit matters in everyday life.

The brand is deliberately less aggressive than low-cost challenger brands and less formal than traditional financial or insurance brands.

## Tone

Prefer:
> “Everything you need for everyday mobile use in Switzerland — plus EU roaming when you travel.”

Avoid:
> “The ultimate revolutionary mobile experience that changes everything.”

## Writing principles

1. Put the customer benefit before the technical specification where possible.
2. Keep claims precise.
3. Do not exaggerate or imply guarantees that are not explicitly supported.
4. Avoid “best”, “#1”, “unbeatable”, “guaranteed”, “always”, “everywhere” and similar superlatives unless the Approved Marketing Claims Register explicitly permits them.
5. Use Swiss spelling conventions in German-language material. English campaign material should remain natural and straightforward.

## Brand positioning

Züricom aims to be known for **straightforward Swiss connectivity**: good everyday service, understandable plans and fewer unpleasant surprises.

The brand should not be positioned as the cheapest provider on the market. Price can be a supporting proof point, but clarity and practical value are stronger brand territories.
