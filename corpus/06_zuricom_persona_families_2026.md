# Züricom Persona — Families

**Owner:** Consumer Marketing Insights  
**Version:** 1.2  
**Effective from:** 1 February 2026  
**Status:** Current

## Who they are

The Family segment includes households managing two or more mobile subscriptions, often with parents responsible for contracts and billing.

## Main needs

- easy account and billing management;
- predictable household costs;
- practical data allowances for different household members;
- clear parental and account controls;
- the ability to add or change lines without administrative hassle.

## Messaging implications

Families respond to simplicity and household value rather than technical specifications.

Good themes include:
- “One household, simpler mobile management.”
- “Keep everyone connected without juggling separate bills.”

Avoid borrowing Frequent Traveler messaging unless the specific campaign is also targeting customers who travel frequently.
