# Züricom Approved Marketing Claims Register — Q3 2026

**Owner:** Marketing Governance & Legal  
**Effective:** 1 July 2026  
**Status:** Current

This register identifies wording approved for recurring consumer campaign use. It does not replace the full product terms.

| Claim ID | Approved wording | Conditions |
|---|---|---|
| U-01 | “CHF 59/month” | Current Unlimited Plan price; no temporary promotion implied. |
| U-02 | “Unlimited mobile data in Switzerland” | Unlimited Plan only. |
| U-03 | “Unlimited calls and SMS in Switzerland” | Standard calls/SMS; exclusions remain. |
| U-04 | “EU roaming included” | Must refer to the defined EU roaming area. |
| U-05 | “Up to three additional Family SIMs for CHF 15/month each” | Must make clear these are additional paid SIMs. |
| U-06 | “One month's notice to cancel” | Do not shorten to “cancel anytime”. |
| U-07 | “Straightforward Swiss connectivity” | General brand positioning statement. |

## Claims requiring additional approval

The following are **not approved as generic claims**:
- “Unlimited roaming everywhere in Europe”;
- “Free roaming worldwide”;
- “Switzerland's best network”;
- “The cheapest unlimited plan”;
- “No hidden fees”;
- “Works exactly like home everywhere”.

These formulations either overstate scope, make a comparative claim without evidence, or imply a guarantee.

## Competitor claims

Direct competitor comparisons must use the latest approved evidence available to the campaign team and must identify the relevant comparison period where pricing or plan features can change.

## Review rule

If a generated sentence is not directly supported by a current product source or an approved claim, treat it as a draft requiring review rather than as an approved marketing claim.
