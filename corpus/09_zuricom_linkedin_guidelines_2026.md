# Züricom LinkedIn Campaign Guidelines

**Owner:** Brand Social & Content  
**Version:** 2.0  
**Effective from:** 15 February 2026  
**Status:** Current

## Purpose

LinkedIn is used for employer brand, corporate communication, selected consumer launches and thought leadership. Consumer campaign posts should remain useful and credible rather than sounding like performance ads.

## Recommended format for consumer launches

- Start with a customer observation, travel moment or useful insight.
- Introduce one clear product benefit.
- Support the benefit with one or two concrete facts.
- Finish with a simple next step or link.

## Style

Aim for approximately **80–150 words** for a standard product post. Shorter posts are acceptable where the message is self-contained.

Use natural, confident language. Emojis should be used sparingly and only where they genuinely improve readability.

## Claims

All factual product and pricing claims must be supported by current product documentation and the Approved Marketing Claims Register.

Do not use:
- unsupported competitor comparisons;
- “#1” or “best network” claims without an approved claim ID;
- vague geographic claims such as “everywhere in Europe” when the offer is limited to a defined roaming area.
