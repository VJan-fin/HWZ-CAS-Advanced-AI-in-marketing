# Züricom Customer Research — Subscription Switching & Price Sensitivity

## Executive summary

Züricom's 2026 switching research indicates that customers compare mobile offers using a combination of monthly price, included services, network confidence and perceived simplicity.

### Key findings

**Price:** 54% of respondents said the headline monthly price is the first thing they check when comparing plans.

**Clarity:** 61% said they become less likely to switch when the offer requires them to read several pages of exceptions or tariff tables.

**Trust:** 47% reported that exaggerated advertising claims reduce their confidence in the provider even when the underlying offer is competitive.

**Switching friction:** Customers value clear cancellation and number-porting information. They are particularly skeptical of promotional prices that revert to materially higher standard prices after a short period.

## Segment observations

Frequent travelers are somewhat less price-sensitive than occasional travelers when the roaming offer is easy to understand and clearly relevant to their trips.

Families put more weight on account simplicity and the price of additional lines.

Young professionals place relatively high value on flexibility and digital self-service.

## Marketing implications

For campaign strategy, use the research to explain **why** a benefit may matter, but use product documents to determine **what** Züricom actually offers.
