# Züricom Unlimited Plan — Product Fact Sheet

**Owner:** Consumer Mobile Product Management  
**Version:** 3.1  
**Effective from:** 15 June 2026  
**Status:** Current

## Product overview

The Züricom Unlimited Plan is the company's main Swiss premium mobile subscription for customers who want predictable everyday mobile use in Switzerland without a monthly data cap.

**Monthly subscription fee:** CHF 59/month.

The plan includes:
- unlimited mobile data in Switzerland;
- unlimited standard calls within Switzerland;
- unlimited standard SMS within Switzerland;
- 5G access on compatible devices and in locations covered by the Züricom 5G network;
- EU roaming as described in the separate Roaming & International Usage Policy.

Premium-rate services, special numbers and other chargeable third-party services are not included.

## Cancellation and contract terms

Customers can cancel the Unlimited Plan with **one month's notice**. The plan does not have a fixed minimum contract period for the subscription itself.

Do not describe this as “same-day cancellation” or “cancel anytime with no notice”. The one-month notice period remains applicable.

## Fair-use principle

The subscription is intended primarily for normal personal use in Switzerland, with ordinary temporary travel abroad. It is not intended for continuous or primarily foreign use. Züricom may take reasonable measures if usage significantly deviates from normal consumer use or indicates that the SIM is being used mainly outside Switzerland.

## Family SIMs

The Unlimited Plan can be combined with **up to three additional Family SIMs**. Each additional Family SIM costs **CHF 15/month** and shares the main subscription's Swiss data allowance.

Do not present Family SIMs as included in the CHF 59 base price.

## Roaming summary

Customers should refer to the current Roaming & International Usage Policy for the detailed country list and treatment of data, calls and SMS while abroad.

For campaign copy, it is acceptable to state that the plan includes roaming benefits in **EU countries**, provided the geographic qualifier is retained.

## Marketing notes

Primary product strengths:
1. predictable CHF 59 monthly price;
2. unlimited use in Switzerland;
3. included EU roaming;
4. simple cancellation terms;
5. option to add up to three Family SIMs.

The product should be positioned as straightforward and dependable rather than as a “luxury” or “exclusive” subscription.
