# Züricom Roaming & International Usage Policy

**Owner:** Consumer Mobile Tariff Management  
**Version:** 2.4  
**Effective from:** 15 June 2026  
**Status:** Current

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the treatment of mobile data, calls and SMS when Züricom customers use their subscription outside Switzerland. It is the controlling source for customer-facing statements about included roaming on current consumer plans.

## 2. Unlimited Plan — EU roaming

For customers on the current Unlimited Plan, mobile data, standard calls and SMS are included while travelling in **EU countries** covered by Züricom's roaming agreement.

For marketing purposes, “EU roaming included” is an acceptable summary. “Roaming included throughout Europe” is **not** an approved equivalent because Europe is broader than the defined EU roaming area.

## 3. Outside the EU

Outside the EU roaming area, standard international roaming tariffs apply unless the customer has an applicable roaming option or package.

The Unlimited Plan should therefore not be advertised as providing unrestricted or free roaming worldwide.

## 4. Country scope

The standard EU roaming area includes the EU member states used in Züricom's current roaming configuration. The United Kingdom, Switzerland and other non-EU territories are handled according to the applicable country/zone tariff and should not automatically be described as “EU roaming”.

For a complete country-level answer, use the country tariff table rather than relying on the phrase “Europe”.

## 5. Travel Add-on

The Travel Add-on is intended primarily for eligible plans that do not already include the relevant roaming benefit. The current eligibility and price are maintained in the Travel Add-on product note.

The Travel Add-on should not be presented as an extra purchase required by Unlimited Plan customers for ordinary EU travel unless a current product rule explicitly says so.

## 6. Claims requiring care

Avoid:
- “roaming is free everywhere in Europe”;
- “unlimited data abroad”;
- “no roaming charges worldwide”;
- “use your plan everywhere exactly like at home”.

Preferred wording is precise about geography, eligibility and the services included.
